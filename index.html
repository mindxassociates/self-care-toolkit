Seven anchors for managing stress

Stress management is not eliminating discomfort; it is strengthening your capacity to cope, recover, and act.

🧭

Reframe stress

Stress is not automatically bad. It can focus attention and motivate problem-solving. The aim is workable stress, not zero stress.

💬

Talk it through

Even when a problem cannot be solved immediately, talking with someone you trust can reduce isolation and emotional load.

✅

Prioritize deliberately

Complete one or two quick, clear tasks to create mental space for larger responsibilities. Busy does not mean everything must happen now.

🌱

Return to the basics

Stress can disrupt food, sleep, movement, and rest—and those gaps create more stress. Supporting your body is often the best first move.

⚖️

Build more than one support

Invest across work, relationships, interests, physical health, and growth. Multiple sources of meaning create resilience when one area is strained.

🕰️

Protect personal time

Schedule rest and enjoyment instead of waiting until everything is done. Recovery is part of functioning, not a prize for productivity.

🔭

Zoom out

Ask: Will this matter in a week? In a year? Writing it down can separate facts, predictions, and the next step you can take.
